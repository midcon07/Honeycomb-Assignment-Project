Every layout

Verified hardware

The axis and detent numbers under each lever are measured, not assumed: positions 1–6 report HID Y X Rz Ry Rx Z, with reverse-detent buttons 24 25 26 27 28 33. Those buttons are what make the reverser check possible — the axis reads 0 on both sides of the gate, so the button is the only signal that a lever is in reverse.

Layouts are yours, mapping is mine

The eleven layouts come from your spec. Which aircraft belongs to which layout is my guess for demo purposes, and is the part meant to be derived from the sim rather than hand-written.

Shape as well as colour

Every handle is drawn to its own silhouette — flat black throttle blocks, ribbed blue prop heads, domed red mixture heads, waisted white jet throttles, the tall SPEED BRAKE blade and the squat FLAP block. Shape says as much as colour, so the card still reads in poor light.
